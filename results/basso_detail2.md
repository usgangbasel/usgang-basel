## INFOS
The daydance series opens the winter season. Doors at 16:00, light out early, the room carries the rest.
  

Britta Arnold
Berlin. Playing since 2009, house with a melodic pull. Releases on Katermukke, Bar 25 and Dantze, at home between Kater Blau and Watergate.
↳ [_soundcloud.com/britta-1_](https://soundcloud.com/britta-1)
  

Vornelinks
Basel duo. Indie dance and house, no strangers to this room.
↳ [_soundcloud.com/vornelinks-dj_](https://soundcloud.com/vornelinks-dj)
  

Marlon & Floyd
Basel. House, last seen at Nordstern.
↳ [_soundcloud.com/malodimusic_](https://soundcloud.com/malodimusic)
  

Kali Karacho
Between Freiburg and Basel. Genre fluid, indie dance into house and techno.
↳ [_soundcloud.com/kalikaracho_](https://soundcloud.com/kalikaracho)
  

Midtempo • Deep House • Indie Dance • House
  

Saturday, 17.10.26
16:00 / RESTI
Elsässerrheinweg 101, Basel
Presale from CHF 12 / Door CHF 25
